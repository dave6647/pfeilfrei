Arrow Escape is a calm arrow puzzle in the style of a metro map.

Every arrow can only move in the direction it points. Tap an arrow: if its path is clear, it glides off the board. If another arrow is in the way, they crash – and that costs a heart. Find the right order and clear the whole board.

THREE MODES
• Classic – 3 hearts per level. The boards grow and get trickier, slowly and without end.
• Hardcore – 5 hearts for all levels together. Five crashes and you start over at level 1. How far can you get?
• Blitz – random levels against the clock. Every solved level adds time, every crash costs 5 seconds.
