Clear the Line

Arrow puzzle for Android

Every arrow moves in one direction only. Send them all off the board in the right order without a crash.

Three modes

Classic: 3 hearts per level, levels that slowly get harder without end.
Hardcore: 5 hearts for all levels together.
Blitz: random levels against the clock.

No ads, no account, no tracking. Play offline in English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Indonesian and Turkish.
